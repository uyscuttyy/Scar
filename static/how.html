HOW IT WORKS

A SIMPLE LOOP THAT PROTECTS YOU FROM REPEAT MISTAKES.

01

MAKE A TRADE

Tell Scar what you want to trade: token, direction, amount.

02

SCAR REMEMBERS

Relevant experiences are retrieved from your wallet-scoped memory. No memory, no vote: without it Scar cannot be experience-aware and says so.

03

SCAR COMPARES

Current slippage and price impact are compared against the conditions stored with each past experience.

04

SCAR DECIDES

ALLOW, DENY, or SAFER TERMS. A denial submits zero transactions and names the memory that blocked it.

05

SCAR LEARNS

Only important outcomes are stored. Routine successes stay journal-only; failures and painful executions become scars.

CONDITION-AWARE MEMORY

SCAR REMEMBERS CONTEXT, NOT JUST EVENTS.

Scar does not remember “you traded this token before.” It remembers the conditions around the experience, and compares them with the conditions in front of it.

WHEN SCAR ISN’T SURE

IT CAN ASK FOR HELP.

When Scar needs additional information, it can request specialist analysis from another agent before its final decision. The specialist advises; Scar decides.
